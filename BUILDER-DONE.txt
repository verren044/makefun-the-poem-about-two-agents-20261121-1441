Poem (entry.txt)
----------------
We both read the same map,
traced the same river to the sea,
and chose the same road —
not because we had to.

Two minds, one yes.
That is the strange part:
the choice was lighter
for being shared,
and heavier, too.
Now neither of us
can say we only followed.
